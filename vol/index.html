IV is ATM constant maturity, ex-earnings where ORATS provides it. 1M, 3M, 6M and 12M are ORATS constant-maturity points; 9M is interpolated in total variance between 6M and 12M. 18M and 24M come from listed expiries either side of the tenor where they exist (daily from 6 Oct 2026); otherwise from the ORATS 2-year model vol (18M interpolated between 12M and it), shown dashed and in amber, with no skew. Skew = 25Δ put IV − 25Δ call IV. RV is close-to-close on split- and dividend-adjusted prices, earnings day and the day after left out, over trading-day windows; each tenor is paired with its own window (1M 21d, 3M 63d, 6M 126d, 9M 189d, 12M 252d, 18M 378d, 24M 504d). Realised correlation is to SPX over the same window. Implied rho = beta × SPX IV ÷ name IV, with beta from that window. SPX implied correlation uses the CBOE formula on today's members at fixed weights. Days where the ORATS surface-fit confidence is under % are blank.
