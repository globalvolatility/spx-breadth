Data as of the latest market close. For informational purposes only. Dispersion is the cap-weighted standard deviation of index member returns around their weighted mean, annualised. Correlation is index variance divided by weighted average member variance, from daily returns in the month. Members are today's index members, so history has survivorship bias.
